A video can stay in the Top 3 for at most 2 days in a row before making room for something newer. Ranks 4 and below are filled from the same pool, ordered by views-per-day. On most shelves a second search limited to the last week is mixed in, so new uploads can reach the shelf on their first day, and a video that has already spent 4 days on a shelf moves to the back of the list (it only stays if nothing newer can take its place). Exactly what was used today is shown under each shelf's heading.
Vertical short-form videos are excluded (they don't fit the 16:9 cards). NEW = wasn't on this shelf yesterday. Mass-produced narration channels are removed from the rankings as we find them.
No more than 3 videos per channel on any one shelf, so one creator can't fill the whole list — the Top 3 spotlights are always from different channels.
Videos play on YouTube itself. This site does not host, download or re-stream any video.
